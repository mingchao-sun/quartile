# Privacy Policy — Quartile

Quartile does not collect, sell or transmit personal data.

- **Stored on your device only:** your language choice, the list of data tables you enabled, the author names you entered, and cached results (in `chrome.storage.local`). Clear them any time from the options page or by removing the extension.
- **Network requests:** the extension sends paper DOIs or titles and journal ISSNs to `api.openalex.org` to fetch citation and journal data, and downloads public CSV files from `raw.githubusercontent.com` (the ShowJCR repository). No account, cookie or browsing history is sent.
- **No analytics, no tracking, no advertising.**

Third-party services have their own privacy policies: OpenAlex (https://openalex.org) and GitHub (https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies).
